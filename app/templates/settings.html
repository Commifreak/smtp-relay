Sender rewriting

mailbox.org only accepts sender addresses that belong to your account (primary address, alias or own domain). Rewriting lets internal services with other sender addresses deliver anyway.

The original From header is always kept as X-Original-From.

Queue

Every message is stored first and delivered immediately. If the relay rejects it permanently (5xx), the client receives the error directly. On temporary failures (relay unreachable, 4xx) the message is accepted and retried automatically.
