Queue

Every message is stored first and delivered immediately. If the relay rejects it permanently (5xx), the client receives the error directly. On temporary failures (relay unreachable, 4xx) the message is accepted and retried automatically.

smtp-relay sends an alert mail when something needs your attention. Alerts go through the queue like any other mail and appear in the mail history.

Alerts are sent as “smtp-relay <address>”. Without a sender address, the relay's sender rewriting address is used, otherwise the relay username if it is an email address, otherwise the alert address itself. The relay's sender rewriting is not applied to alerts.
